A little hunt leading to a big upgrade — a Single Occupancy room at Grand Hyatt Kochi.

Why this exists

The Sangamam 2026 offsite has an extra Single Occupancy Room at Grand Hyatt Kochi, Bolgatty Island. We could give it away in a raffle — but that felt boring.

Instead, we built a quick 10-mission upgrade challenge. Anyone in the firm can play, and whoever cracks all 10 missions with the highest score in the fastest time wins the upgrade. Simple.

The Story

🌊 You've just landed in Kochi for Sangamam 2026 — but there's one problem. Your Hyatt room is locked.

Complete 10 missions across the airport, the taxi ride, the backwaters, the hotel corridors, and a few mind-benders. Each mission unlocks one secret clue. Combine all 10 clues in order to unlock your room.

Rules

  • ⏱5 minutes total on the clock, from the moment you hit "Begin"
  • 🎯10 missions · 10 points each · 100 points max
  • 🔁3 attempts per mission — with scaling points:
    Try 1 = 10 pts Try 2 = 6 pts Try 3 = 3 pts All wrong = 0 pts
  • ⏭If you use all 3 attempts, we'll reveal the correct answer and move you to the next mission — so nobody gets stuck
  • 🔑Each mission unlocks one clue character. All 10 combined = the room's unlock code
  • 🏆Ranking: highest score → fastest time → earliest submission wins
  • 📧One attempt per Bain email. Play carefully — you only get one shot
